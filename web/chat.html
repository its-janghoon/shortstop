Moving house Ask 2
Make me a short packing list for a two-bedroom move.
Start with boxes, tape and labels. Pack one room at a time, keep a bag with chargers, papers and a change of clothes, and photograph the meter readings on moving day.
I'm moving next month and just got the lease. Can you help me read it?
Happy to. Paste the lease and I'll pull out the rent, the deposit and anything unusual.
What should I ask the landlord before I sign?
Three good ones:
  • Who pays for repairs, and how fast are they done?
  • When and how is the deposit returned?
  • Can I end the lease early, and what does it cost?
Ask can make mistakes.
